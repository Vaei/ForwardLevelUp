What you get

Smooth edges

MSAA with a wider, smoother resolve. Thin railings, poles and wires stop shimmering.

No halos

Shadows and AO no longer bleed across MSAA edges.

AO that works in forward

Screen-space, distance-field and street-scale occlusion from buildings, all of it MSAA-aware.

Contact shadows

Characters darken the ground under them, using the engine's capsule shadows.

Reflections

Screen-space reflections work alongside MSAA.

World normals

Post-process materials can read SceneTexture WorldNormal in forward.

Decals in the base pass

Decals change base colour, normal and roughness before lighting, so every light shades them, at every MSAA sample.

Bounce light

Coloured light bounced off the buildings and street around you, from the same height capture as the occlusion.

Contact shadows for anything

Vehicles and props darken the ground under them too, without distance fields.

Clean soft particles

Depth-faded particles stop leaving a halo around foreground edges.

In motion

Aliasing is worst in motion. The same gameplay before and after.

Set it up

You need a UE 5.8 source build, and a project on the mobile renderer: bPreferFeatureLevelES31 on PC, or a mobile platform.

  1. Download the repository and apply the fourteen patches in Patches/ to the engine, in order, with git apply.
  2. Build the editor. The first launch recompiles every material.
  3. Add the settings below to Config/DefaultEngine.ini under [/Script/Engine.RendererSettings], then restart the editor.
r.Mobile.ShadingPath=0
r.Mobile.AntiAliasing=3
r.MSAACount=4
r.WideCustomResolve=2
r.Mobile.MSAA.EdgeAwareFetch=1
r.Mobile.MSAA.SkipRedundantResolve=1
r.Mobile.AmbientOcclusion=1
r.DefaultFeature.AmbientOcclusion=True

The README covers the optional extras (distance-field AO, contact shadows, reflections, occlusion and bounce light from buildings, decals, soft particles, world normals), what to check when something doesn't show up, and every setting.

Under the hood

What each patch changes
PatchStock 5.8Patched
1 Wide resolveMobile gets a 1-pixel box filter; the wider resolve only compiles for desktop.The wider resolve runs on the mobile renderer (r.WideCustomResolve).
2 Distance-field shadow fixThe distance-field shadow pass is dropped under MSAA, with an ensure at startup.Works under MSAA.
3 Edge fetch and AOShadows and AO bleed across MSAA edges. Forward reads AO from a channel nothing writes, and mobile SSAO never runs on PC.Edge samples fetch from the matching surface (r.Mobile.MSAA.EdgeAwareFetch). Forward gets SSAO.
4 Skip resolveA hardware resolve runs that the custom resolve then overwrites.Optionally skipped (r.Mobile.MSAA.SkipRedundantResolve). Identical output.
5 Forward distance-field AOMobile distance-field AO only works in deferred.Works in forward (r.Mobile.DistanceFieldAO).
6 Contact shadows and SSR fixCapsule sky occlusion is disabled on mobile. Mobile SSR crashes with patch 4.Capsule occlusion on mobile, optionally cast straight down (r.CapsuleIndirectSkyLightFromAbove). SSR safe under MSAA.
7 World normalsForward has no normal buffer, so post-process materials cannot read WorldNormal.Optional normal target (r.Mobile.Forward.NormalTarget).
8 Occlusion from buildingsNothing occludes the sky beyond a few metres.Sky occlusion from a top-down height capture of the level (r.Mobile.HeightFieldAO).
9 Tonemap subpass resolveThe on-chip tonemap subpass averages HDR samples, then tonemaps, so bright edges stair-step.Weighted or per-sample tonemapped resolve (r.Mobile.TonemapSubpass.Resolve).
10 Per-sample depth fadeTranslucency fades against the resolved foreground depth, leaving halos at edges.Reads the MSAA sample behind the fragment (r.Mobile.MSAA.PerSampleDepthFade).
11 Primitive AOContact occlusion needs distance fields, and only characters cast it without them.Capsules and bounds ellipsoids for any movable (r.Mobile.PrimitiveAO).
12 Height-field clipmapOne capture of the whole level.A toroidal window that follows the camera, captured a few tiles per frame.
13 Height-field GINo bounce light without baked lighting.One bounce off the height field (r.Mobile.HeightFieldGI).
14 Clustered decalsForward decals draw over scene colour with their own lighting, no normals or roughness, no local lights.Baked into an atlas and applied in the base pass from screen tile lists (r.Mobile.ClusteredDecals).
